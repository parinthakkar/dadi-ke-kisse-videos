# Privacy Policy – Dadi Ke Kisse Uploader

Dadi Ke Kisse Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Dadi Ke Kisse" YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos.

- It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, to upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to playlists.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or other users.
- Access credentials are stored privately as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are used only for uploading.
- The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the channel owner, via the email listed on the Google consent screen.
